A family‑owned dealership in the Chicagoland area known for premium customer service, Italian performance motorcycles, a friendly team, and strong community involvement. This role became a pivotal chapter in my growth, giving me a firsthand understanding of the immense work and dedication required to run a small business. It deepened my knowledge of backend products, dealership operations, and—through increased responsibility—helped me step into a more impactful, contributor‑level role. My background in marketing, design, and event production is also reflected throughout the work showcased here.

Aprilia demo event/open house

This event was an exclusive opportunity to test ride rare, premium, high- performance sport bikes made by the Italian motorcycle manufacturer Aprilia. The brand is well known in the MotoGP and MotoAmerica race world, but very little is known about it through the general population that does not follow supersport motorcycle racing. This event drew in over 160 riders and as a result indirectly sold over 40 Aprilia motorcycles measured via registrations.



open house & UNVEILING

The unveiling of the Moto Guzzi V100 Mandello was long awaited. It was released in Europe an entire year before the United States. The event brought out dozens of enthusiasts and several new riders foreign to the brand. The unveiling was coupled with an Open House and we fed everyone a free spaghetti and meatball dinner. People traveled from other states just to see the new lineup and more importantly to see the new unveiling . It was an incredible opportunity for us to grow the brand and increase floor traffic in the dealership. As a result we were able to sell several of these models. Sadly, we were limited by small production levels or we potentially could have sold more Guzzi's.
