ANNUAL HONOR FLIGHT RIDE

Every Spring we collaborated to create this annual ride that left our dealership and headed out to the runway at the DuPage County Airport for a charity event to support and honor remaining WW2 and Vietnam War veterans in Washington D.C. It features war era vehicles, planes, uniformed veterans, a large luncheon, raffles, and tons of history. It's always been the "feel good event of the year" and deserves a place here cemented in time.


15TH ANNUAL STURGIS IN LISLE CELEBRATION

Once a year during the world's largest motorcycle rally we host this festival-like celebration. It takes months of work in planning and marketing and is all over in one day. The event is always compliant with safety regulations and city ordinances. The event would draw hundreds of bikers throughout the day making it the largest event of the year annually with free food, plenty of live entertainment, raffle prizes, vendors, and $1.00 beers.

110 yr. ann. miller/h-d bike giveaway

During the 110th year anniversary Miller Lite came to us and wanted to do a co-op event to give away a Harley-Davidson. This event turned out to be a large success as it helped us get customers out to other events in and outside of the dealership.
